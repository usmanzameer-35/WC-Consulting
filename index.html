04 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Which service should I start with?+

Choose the Clarity Review if your records or priorities are unclear. Choose Project Finance Control when you need visibility around a specific project. Choose Monthly Finance Partner for a regular management reporting cycle.

How are fees determined?+

Fees are scoped around the number of entities and projects, transaction volume, record quality, reporting frequency and the work required. A proposal should define deliverables, responsibilities and fees before work starts.

What information is needed?+

Depending on scope: management accounts or a trial balance, receivables and payables reports, bank records, project budgets, purchase commitments and client billing schedules. The required records are agreed during scoping.

Can you work with our existing accountant?+

The engagement can complement an internal accountant or external accounting provider. Agree who maintains the books, who supplies each report and who approves financial decisions.

Does this include audit, tax or investment advice?+

This offer focuses on accounting operations, project reporting and financial control. Statutory audit, tax representation, investment advice and legal services are outside the described scope.
